# Contributing to MillionSend

Thanks for your interest. MillionSend is pre-launch and moving fast — this document will grow
as the codebase does.

## Where things happen

- **Questions & ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/MillionSend/discussions) —
  our primary support and conversation surface.
- **Bugs & concrete feature requests:** issues on the relevant repo (please use the issue forms).
- **Security problems:** never a public issue — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Pull requests

- Open an issue or discussion before large changes so we can agree on direction first.
- PR titles follow [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org)
  (`feat: …`, `fix: …`, `docs: …`) — we squash-merge, so your PR title becomes the commit.
- Keep PRs focused; small is fast.

## Licensing of contributions

- SDK repositories are MIT: contributions land under MIT (inbound = outbound).
- The product repository is AGPL-3.0. A lightweight Contributor License Agreement will be
  requested automatically by a bot on your first PR there — it's a one-time comment,
  signatures are stored in the repository.

## Code of conduct

Everyone participating is expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
